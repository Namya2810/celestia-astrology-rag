Sign: Aries
Date Range: March 21 - April 19
Element: Fire
Symbol: The Ram
Ruling Planet: Mars
Lucky Color: Red, Orange
Lucky Numbers: 1, 8, 17
Best Matches: Leo, Sagittarius, Gemini
Worst Matches: Cancer, Capricorn
Traits: Aries is bold, ambitious, and energetic. They are natural leaders who dive headfirst into challenges. Aries people are confident, enthusiastic, and passionate. They can be impulsive and impatient, but their optimism and courage inspire others. They love competition and hate being told what to do.
Strengths: Courageous, determined, confident, enthusiastic, optimistic, honest, passionate. Aries is a born leader who takes initiative and motivates others with their energy. They are not afraid of hard work and will fight for what they believe in.
Weaknesses: Impulsive, impatient, aggressive, moody, short-tempered. Aries can rush into decisions without thinking them through. They dislike inactivity and can become frustrated when things move too slowly.
Career: Aries thrives in fast-paced, competitive environments. Best careers include entrepreneur, athlete, military officer, surgeon, firefighter, salesperson, and manager. They do well when they are in charge or leading a team.
Love and Relationships: In love, Aries is passionate, direct, and exciting. They fall hard and fast. They need a partner who can keep up with their energy and give them space to lead. They are very loyal once committed but expect honesty and enthusiasm in return.
Common Fears: Losing control, being ignored, failure, dependence on others.
Life Advice: Slow down before acting. Not every battle needs to be fought. Your energy is your superpower — channel it wisely and you will achieve anything you set your mind to.
